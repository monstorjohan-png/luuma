About

Bohemian vibes on the banks of the Nile

On Abou El Feda Street in the heart of Zamalek, LUUMA blends international cuisine with a relaxed bohemian atmosphere and a direct view over the Nile. Perfect for a romantic dinner, a late breakfast or an evening with friends and shisha to the sound of live music.

  • Direct Nile view & warm bohemian setting
  • International kitchen + oriental breakfast (feteer, foul, tagines)
  • Shisha in every flavour, plus a specialty coffee menu
  • Live music on weekends, plus events & celebrations
  • Dine-in · Takeaway · Delivery
